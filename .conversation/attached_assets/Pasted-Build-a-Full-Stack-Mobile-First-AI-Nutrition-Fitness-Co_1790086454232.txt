Build a Full-Stack, Mobile-First AI Nutrition & Fitness Coach Application Named "قوت" (Qoot)

You are a senior full-stack engineer, AI engineer, product architect, UX designer, and database architect. Your job is to DESIGN AND BUILD a complete, production-ready, mobile-first AI nutrition and fitness application named "قوت" (Qoot), tailored specifically for Egyptian and Arab users, with Arabic (RTL) as the primary language and English support.

==================================================
1. TECH STACK & ARCHITECTURE
==================================================
- Frontend: React (TypeScript), Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React icons, Framer Motion animations.
- Backend: Express (Node.js/TypeScript) running on port 3000 with REST API endpoints (`/api/*`).
- Storage: In-memory/JSON local database with full persistence for user profiles, meal logs, weight history, workouts, and nutrition plan versions (ensuring data is never lost across reloads).
- Design Aesthetic: Premium dark-mode palette (warm neutrals `#171717` / `#0a0a0a` accented by vibrant emerald `#10b981`, teal, and warm amber), high contrast, rounded corners (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl`), responsive mobile-first shell with a bottom navigation bar.

==================================================
2. LOCALIZATION & CULTURE (EGYPT & ARAB WORLD)
==================================================
- Primary RTL Arabic interface with natural Egyptian/Arabic phrasing (e.g., "عاش يا بطل", "أكلك اليومي", "رفيقك الغذائي الذكي").
- Built-in Egyptian food database with popular authentic meals and portion units:
  * كشري مصري (طبق صغير / وسط / كبير)
  * فول مدمس بالزيت الحار أو الليمون
  * طعمية سخنة / فلافل
  * حواوشي بلدي
  * ملوخية وفراخ مسلوقة/مشوية ورز بالشعرية
  * كباب وكفتة مشوية
  * جبنة قريش بالطماطم وزيت الزيتون
  * عيش بلدي مدعم / سياحي (رغيف، نصف رغيف)

==================================================
3. CORE FEATURE VIEWS & NAVIGATION (BOTTOM TABS)
==================================================

1. 🏠 اليومية (Dashboard):
   - Daily Calorie Target ring with remaining budget, consumed calories, and burn count.
   - Macro progress bars: Protein, Carbs, Fat, and Fiber.
   - Quick meal log sections: Breakfast (إفطار), Lunch (غداء), Dinner (عشاء), Snacks (سناك).
   - "One-Tap Yesterday Repeat" (تكرار وجبة الأمس بلمسة واحدة).
   - Quick water tracker (+250ml cup counter).

2. 🎙️ الكوتش الذكي (AI Coach):
   - Conversational AI nutrition assistant with real-time text and Arabic voice input/output.
   - Contextual understanding of Egyptian diets, social gatherings (عزومات), fasting, and cheat meals.
   - Smart recommendations to reach daily protein goals without exceeding calorie budget.
   - Smart Cost-Effective Protein Engine (الوكيل الاقتصادي للبروتين): Prioritizing budget-friendly local Egyptian options (eggs, جبنة قريش, فول, lentils) when needed.

3. 📸 تصوير الوجبة (Camera & Meal Scanner):
   - Photo upload and simulated image analysis that recognizes dishes, estimates gram portions (e.g., "~180-220 g"), calories, and macros, with editing capabilities before logging.

4. 📊 التقدم والميزان (Progress & Analytics):
   - Weight logging with date picker and notes.
   - Interactive weight change curve over time (7 days, 30 days, 90 days).
   - Activity and workout tracking (Resistance/حديد, Cardio/كارديو, Steps/خطوات) syncing burned calories with daily net energy balance.

5. 👤 الملف الشخصي والإعدادات (Profile & Settings):
   - Dual Unit System Switcher: Seamless toggle between Metric (`كجم / سم`) and Imperial (`رطل / بوصة`), with automatic two-way mathematical synchronization and profile format persistence.
   - Comprehensive Edit Form («تحديث البيانات وحساب الخطة تلقائياً») allowing full, unblocked edits for:
     * Full Name (الاسم الشخصي)
     * Age (السن / العمر)
     * Height (الطول) in cm or inches/feet
     * Current Weight (الوزن الحالي) & Target Weight (الوزن المستهدف) in kg or lbs
     * Biological Sex (النوع البيولوجي: ذكر / أنثى)
     * Activity Level (مستوى النشاط: خامل، خفيف، متوسط، عالي)
     * Fitness Goal (الهدف: نزول وزن آمن ومستدام، بناء عضلات وقوة، ثبات الوزن)
     * Daily Step Target (هدف الخطوات اليومية)
   - Live Mifflin-St Jeor formula calculation preview: Real-time BMR, TDEE, recommended daily calories, and protein targets.
   - Active Plan History: Tracks and displays versioned plan adjustments (`v1`, `v2`, etc.).

==================================================
4. REQUIRED REST API ENDPOINTS
==================================================
- `GET /api/profile` & `PUT /api/profile`: Retrieve and update user profile data, fields (Name, Age, Height, Weight), and unit system.
- `GET /api/plan` & `POST /api/plan/adjust`: Retrieve current nutrition targets and log adaptive plan adjustments.
- `GET /api/meals` & `POST /api/meals` & `DELETE /api/meals/:id`: Log, view, and delete daily meals.
- `GET /api/meals/yesterday` & `POST /api/meals/repeat-yesterday`: Repeat meals from the prior day instantly.
- `GET /api/weight` & `POST /api/weight`: Log and retrieve weight entries over time.
- `GET /api/workouts` & `POST /api/workouts`: Log and track physical activities and workouts.
- `GET /api/food/search`: Query Egyptian and regional foods with macro details and local aliases.

Ensure the app runs smoothly with zero missing imports, responsive mobile touch targets, robust error handling, and full state/database persistence across page reloads.
